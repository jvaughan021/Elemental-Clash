How to Play & Rules

Elemental Clash is a tactical 1v1 roguelike card battle game created by Jack Vaughan. Conquer the Tutorial and 50 increasingly difficult bosses to win!

⚡ Core Combat & Energy:

Each turn you have limited Energy to spend playing cards from your hand. Click cards to play them, then click "End Turn" when you are out of moves or want to pass to the boss.

🔥 Elemental Advantage & Boss Counter-Attacks:

Pay close attention to elements! If your card has an elemental advantage over the boss, your attack deals +40% bonus damage. However, if you play a card whose element the boss counters, your damage is reduced to 70% and the boss will immediately retaliate and strike you back right on your turn!

📈 The Fusion Ladder & Drafting:

Bosses only offer Tier-1 Common cards when you defeat them. To get stronger cards, collect 2 identical copies of a card in your deck and take them to the Card Fusion Forge during the intermission shop to upgrade them through the tiers: Common ➔ Uncommon ➔ Rare ➔ Epic ➔ Legendary!

💾 Saving Progress:

Your run automatically saves after every victory, draft, or shop purchase. You can safely close your browser and resume right where you left off anytime!
