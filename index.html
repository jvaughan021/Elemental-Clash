How to Play & Rules

Elemental Clash is a tactical 1v1 card battle game created by Jack Vaughan.

🪙 Battle Points (BP) & Revives:

  • Earn exactly 50 BP by defeating each boss to buy shop perks.
  • You can only choose one shop perk per level (50 BP each).
  • You have 5 total Ad-Powered Revives per run (1 max per level) if defeated!
