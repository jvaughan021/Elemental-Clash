How to Play & Rules

Elemental Clash is a tactical 1v1 roguelike card battle game created by Jack Vaughan. Conquer the Tutorial and 50 increasingly difficult bosses to win!

⚡ Core Combat & Energy:

Each turn you have limited Energy to spend playing cards from your hand. Click cards to play them, then click "End Turn" when you are out of moves or pass to the boss.

🔥 Anti-Spam & Element Fatigue:

Playing multiple cards of the same element in a single turn triggers Boss Anticipation, reducing your damage! Mix up your elements or risk getting countered.

🏦 High-Stakes Royal Vault:

Deposit BP into the bank vault to earn offline compounding interest. Warning: Dying in combat wipes your vault balance and upgrades back to zero!

💾 Saving Progress:

Your run automatically saves after every victory, draft, or shop purchase. You can resume right where you left off anytime!
