How to Play & Rules

Elemental Clash is a tactical 1v1 card battle game created by Jack Vaughan.

🔥 6-Element System (+40% Damage):

  • 🔥 Fire > 🌿 Earth > 💧 Water > 🔥 Fire
  • 🌪️ Air > 🌫️ Spirit > 🌑 Shadow > 🌪️ Air

🪙 Battle Points (BP) & Intermission Shop:

  • Earn 50 BP per boss win. Spend it in the Intermission Shop on Max Energy, Relics, Cards, or Max HP!
  • You have 5 total Ad-Powered Revives per run if defeated!
