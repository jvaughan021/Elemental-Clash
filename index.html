How to Play & Rules

Elemental Clash is a tactical 1v1 card battle game created by Jack Vaughan.

🔥 Elemental Rock-Paper-Scissors:

  • Fire beats Earth (deals 1.4x damage).
  • Earth beats Water (deals 1.4x damage).
  • Water beats Fire (deals 1.4x damage).
  • Shield and Heal cards protect and restore HP.

⚔️ Core Rules & Permadeath:

  • 20 Progressive Levels: Level 1 is very easy, scaling up to Level 20 (Apex Voidlord).
  • Hardcore Permadeath: If your HP drops to 0, restart from Level 1 (Revive Token can only be used once per run!).
  • Full Health Restore: Defeating a boss fully heals your HP to 100 for the next stage.
  • Unique Hand: Your hand automatically ensures no duplicate cards appear simultaneously.
